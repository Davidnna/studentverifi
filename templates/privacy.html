PRIVACY AND RESPONSIBLE USE

Privacy considerations

Identity systems should collect and expose only the information needed for an authorised purpose.

Data minimisation

The prototype uses a QR token to refer to a record rather than putting a student's personal details directly inside the QR code. This reduces what is visible from the code alone, but it does not by itself make the overall system anonymous.

Access to records

Student records and verification history should only be available to authorised users. Authentication, appropriate permissions, secure hosting, and careful handling of credentials are important parts of a responsible deployment.

Academic prototype notice

This page describes design considerations; it is not a complete privacy notice for an operational service. Before real student data is used, the institution should approve the purpose, lawful basis, retention period, access rules, contact point, and relevant data-protection procedures.

Protect your information

  • Do not publish student QR codes or screenshots containing personal information.
  • Do not share account passwords or database credentials.
  • Report suspected unauthorised access to the responsible project or institutional contact.
