ABOUT STUDENTVERIFI

About the project

StudentVerifi is an academic prototype for exploring QR-code-based student identity verification.

Why this project?

Manual identity checks can take time and depend on the availability and accuracy of paper records. This project explores whether a QR-supported workflow can help authorised personnel look up a student record more consistently.

Project objectives

  • Explore the use of unique QR tokens linked to student records.
  • Support status checks against a central database.
  • Provide a record of verification attempts for authorised review.
  • Consider usability, accessibility, privacy, and security in the design.

Prototype status

This is an academic prototype developed for project and demonstration purposes. It should not be represented as an officially adopted or endorsed Ebonyi State University service. Any real-world use would require institutional approval, appropriate data-protection measures, testing, and operational support.
