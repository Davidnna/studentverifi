THE WORKFLOW

How QR-supported verification works

A simple overview of the process explored by this prototype.

1

Student record

An authorised operator creates or maintains a student record in the system.

2

Unique QR token

The system associates the record with a unique token represented by a QR code. Personal details should not be encoded directly into the QR code.

3

Scan or enter

During an authorised check, the token is scanned or entered so the system can look for a matching record.

4

Review the result

The result indicates whether a matching record is found and whether it is active. Staff should also follow approved identity-checking procedures.

Important: A QR code alone does not prove that the person presenting it is the student it represents. An authorised verifier should follow institutional policy and compare identity details where required.
