Frequently asked questions

What is StudentVerifi?

It is an academic prototype exploring QR-code-supported student identity verification.

Is this an official EBSU service?

No. The site is an academic prototype and should not be treated as an official university service or endorsement.

Does a QR code contain a student's personal information?

The intended design uses a unique token to reference a record rather than placing personal details directly in the QR code. A QR code should still be treated carefully because it can be copied or shared.

Does a successful QR scan prove someone's identity?

No. It can show that a token matches a record, but an authorised verifier should follow the institution's identity-checking policy and confirm the person where required.

Can the public search student records here?

No. Public pages provide general project information only. Student records and operational tools are intended for authorised access.

Who should I contact with questions?

Use the project contact information provided by the project owner or academic department. This prototype does not publish a verified support email or telephone number.
